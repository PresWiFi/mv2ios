Move your Android message history, including RCS and group chats, into the Messages app on an iPhone. Everything stays on this computer.

Before you start: the iPhone backup must not be encrypted, and restoring an unencrypted backup loses saved passwords, Health data and call history. The best time to use mv2ios is on a freshly set up iPhone, right after "Move to iOS".

1 Link to your Android phone

mv2ios connects to Google Messages the same way "Messages for Web" does. A browser window opens for you to sign in to the Google account used on the phone.

This computer is linked to Google Messages.

Working…

Your phone is showing three emoji. Tap this one:

On the phone, open Google Messages, tap your profile picture, choose Device pairing, then QR code scanner, and scan:

  1. Open a private window (Incognito in Chrome, InPrivate in Edge) and go to

    Sign in with the Google account used by Google Messages on the phone.
  2. Once the page has loaded, press F12, open the Network tab, and press F5.
  3. Right-click the request named config, choose Copy › Copy as cURL (bash).
  4. Close the private window without signing out, and paste below.

2 Download your messages

Keep the Android phone unlocked, online, and with Google Messages open until this finishes. It can take a long time for big histories. If it stops, run it again and it continues where it left off.

3 Add them to an iPhone backup

Back up the iPhone to this computer with Finder, iTunes or Apple Devices. Leave "Encrypt local backup" turned off. Then pick the backup below.

4 Restore the iPhone

  1. On the iPhone, turn off Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Messages, so iCloud does not overwrite the restored history.
  2. Restore the backup to the iPhone with the same app that made it (Finder, iTunes or Apple Devices).
  3. Check that the messages arrived, then turn iCloud Messages back on if you use it.

Imported RCS messages show as green "Text Message" bubbles. That is expected.
